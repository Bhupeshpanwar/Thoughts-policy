Privacy Policy for Thoughts

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Thoughts app ("Thoughts", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Thoughts is designed to allow users to create notes and audio recordings, including creating notes or recordings from Android notifications and from within the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or transmit personal information from users to our servers.

Notes and audio recordings created using the App are stored locally on the user's device. We do not have access to the content of those notes or recordings.

2. Notification Access

The App may use Android's notification access functionality to read notification content when the user has explicitly enabled notification access for the App.

Notification information is used only to provide the App's note and recording functionality. Notification content is not uploaded to our servers, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Users can disable notification access at any time through their Android device settings.

3. Audio Recordings

If you use the audio recording functionality, the App may request permission to access your device's microphone.

Audio recordings created through the App are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your recordings to our servers.

4. Notes and Local Storage

Notes created through the App are stored locally on your device. The App does not send your notes to us or store them on a remote server.

Because your notes and recordings are stored on your device, deleting the App, clearing its application data, or otherwise removing locally stored data may result in the loss of your notes or recordings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your notes, recordings, notification content, or other personal information with third parties.

6. Advertising

If advertising services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information those services may collect and how it is used.

7. Data Security

Since notes and recordings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers, we do not maintain a remote copy of this information.

You are responsible for securing your device and protecting access to information stored on it.

8. Children's Privacy

Thoughts is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Privacy Policy for Crypto Ticker

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Crypto Ticker app ("Crypto Ticker", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Crypto Ticker lets you choose cryptocurrencies, view their live prices, and optionally show the prices of up to five starred coins in an Android notification.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work properly, the App saves the following information locally on your device only:

  • The cryptocurrencies you selected
  • The coins you starred
  • Whether the price notification is turned on
  • A saved list of available coin names and the most recent prices shown in the notification

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it.

3. Market Data from Binance

The App gets public cryptocurrency market data (coin names and prices) directly from Binance's public API and WebSocket services. Your device connects to Binance directly; the data does not pass through any server of ours.

The App does not use any Binance account or API key, and it does not send your selected coins, starred coins, or any personal information to Binance. As with any internet connection, Binance may receive technical information such as your IP address when your device connects to it. How Binance handles that information is described in Binance's own privacy policy.

4. Notifications

On Android 13 and newer, the App asks for permission to show notifications. This permission is optional and is used only to show the price notification that you turn on. The notification is created on your device from public price data. Its "Reload" button fetches fresh prices from Binance and replaces the notification.

You can turn the notification off inside the App, or block it at any time in your Android device settings.

5. Permissions

The App uses the Internet permission to load market data, and the notification permission described above. The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect it.

7. Advertising

The App does not show advertisements. If advertising is added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information those services may collect.

8. Not Financial Advice

Prices shown in the App come from Binance and can be delayed by a second or more, or be temporarily unavailable. The App is for information only and is not financial, investment, or trading advice. Crypto Ticker cannot buy, sell, or hold cryptocurrency, and it is not a wallet or an exchange. Crypto Ticker is not affiliated with or endorsed by Binance.

9. Children's Privacy

Crypto Ticker is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the App, you can contact us at:
