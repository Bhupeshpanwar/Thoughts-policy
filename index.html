Privacy Policy for Thoughts

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Thoughts app ("Thoughts", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

Thoughts is designed to allow users to create notes and audio recordings, including creating notes or recordings from Android notifications and from within the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or transmit personal information from users to our servers.

Notes and audio recordings created using the App are stored locally on the user's device. We do not have access to the content of those notes or recordings.

2. Notification Access

The App may use Android's notification access functionality to read notification content when the user has explicitly enabled notification access for the App.

Notification information is used only to provide the App's note and recording functionality. Notification content is not uploaded to our servers, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Users can disable notification access at any time through their Android device settings.

3. Audio Recordings

If you use the audio recording functionality, the App may request permission to access your device's microphone.

Audio recordings created through the App are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your recordings to our servers.

4. Notes and Local Storage

Notes created through the App are stored locally on your device. The App does not send your notes to us or store them on a remote server.

Because your notes and recordings are stored on your device, deleting the App, clearing its application data, or otherwise removing locally stored data may result in the loss of your notes or recordings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your notes, recordings, notification content, or other personal information with third parties.

6. Advertising

If advertising services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information those services may collect and how it is used.

7. Data Security

Since notes and recordings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers, we do not maintain a remote copy of this information.

You are responsible for securing your device and protecting access to information stored on it.

8. Children's Privacy

Thoughts is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
